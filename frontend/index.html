What the uncertainty does—and does not—measure

The five partitions reuse the prescribed 100-point dataset, so their test sets overlap. Their standard deviations describe split sensitivity; they are not confidence intervals from independent datasets. The additional 20 shot-resampling trials per condition draw from the saved noisy all-zero probabilities. They quantify conditional measurement variability, not fresh hardware acquisitions. A separate ten-split study compares exact QSVM and classical baselines.

04 / From a point to a prediction

Every claim has a path
back to its evidence.

  1. 01

    Encode

    Fit min/max scaling on training data. Encode two coordinates as (a, b, a, b) across four qubits, with H, RZ and ZZ interactions.

  2. 02

    Estimate

    Apply U(x) and U(x′)†, then count the global 0000 outcome. Estimate 4,840 pairs per kernel, including 80 diagonal diagnostics.

  3. 03

    Compile & correct

    Preserve the ideal unitary while reducing native CX cost. Retain the positive training eigenspace and consistently extend held-out similarities.

  4. 04

    Classify

    Train a classical precomputed-kernel SVM. Freeze the training-selected C across device and compiler conditions; compare with linear and RBF SVMs.

The exact optimization

Compute–uncompute places two diagonal blocks next to each other. Fuse them exactly, then synthesize ring phases using a native parity network.

2r blocks → 2r − 1 blocks

A chain uses 10 CX per block; a native four-cycle uses 8. For the primary two-layer ring, A drops from 62 to 30 CX and B from 30 to 24. Both optimized circuits have depth 52 and zero routing SWAPs.

The limits are part of the result

Four qubits and 100 synthetic points cannot demonstrate quantum speedup. The exact classical RBF baseline is already competitive. Lower gate cost and kernel error do not guarantee better classification.

Noise uses matched depolarizing parameters 0.001 (native single-qubit gates), 0.005 (CX), and independent bit readout error 0.01. Simulator time is not hardware time. No physical quantum processor is accessed.

The secondary linear model costs only 22 CX, but its conditional shot-resampling accuracy is 92% on both devices versus approximately 96% for the optimized ring.
